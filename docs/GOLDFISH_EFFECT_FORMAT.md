# GEF: the Goldfish Effect Format (v0.2)

T1 of the translation track (see `docs/TRANSLATION_T0.md`) defined v0.1; T3 step 1 revised it to v0.2 after the T2 prototype (`docs/TRANSLATION_T2.md`). GEF is a JSON format for what a card does, written once per card (by an LLM translator or by hand) and checked by a machine before anything uses it. Today goldfish.py reads cards with regexes; with GEF, a compiler would turn validated translations into the same engine structures. That compiler is later work.

- Schema: `translation/gef.schema.json`, generated by `translation/gef_schema.py`.
- Vocabulary (every field and value, generated): `translation/vocabulary.md`.
- Validator: `translation/validate.py FILE.json [--status]`, stdlib only.
- Worked examples: `translation/examples/examples.json` (18 cards, all accepted).

## Changes in 0.2

- **No free-text statics.** `replacement`, `restriction` and `hand_ability` are gone. Each T2 use became a construct below, or `unexpressible`:
  - `skip_step {what, who}`: "Skip your draw step."
  - `extra_counters {n, kind, on}`: "that many plus one +1/+1 counters" (Hardened Scales).
  - `graveyard_replacement {whose, to}`: "If a card would be put into your graveyard from anywhere, exile it instead" (`whose: yours, to: exile`); Nexus of Fate is `whose: self, to: library_shuffled`.
  - `discard_to_top {optional, effects_only}`: Library of Leng.
  - `enters_tapped {unless_pay}`: "As this land enters, you may pay 3 life. If you don't, it enters tapped."
  - `spell_limit {n, spells, who}`: Rule of Law (`who: each_player`), Deafening Silence (`spells: {non_types: [creature]}`).
  - `draw_from_empty_library_wins` (was `lab_man`): Laboratory Maniac, Jace, Wielder of Mysteries.
  - `coin_flip_rule {rule}`: Krark's Thumb (`flip_two_ignore_one`), Edgar's Two-Headed Coin (`first_flips_each_turn_win`).
- **Keywords are an enum everywhere** (pump, pump_team, animate, anthem, attached_bonus, tokens, filters, protect). "Can't be blocked" is `unblockable`. A silent keyword with a quality is an object: "protection from red" is `{"keyword": "protection", "detail": "red"}`.
- **`detail` only on silent keywords** (the list is `SILENT_KEYWORDS` in the schema: protection, enchant, partner with, ...). The others say it with fields: `card_filter` for typecycling/landcycling ("Wizardcycling" = `typecycling` with `card_filter: {subtypes: [Wizard]}`), `filter` for equip and offering ("Equip commander {3}" = `equip`, `cost: "{3}"`, `filter: {commander: true}`).
- **The cheap T2 gaps:**
  - counts `total_power` (Klauth: `filter: {attacking: true}`), `permanents_on_battlefield` (every player's), `commander_identity_colors`, `damage_dealt_this_turn` (with `player`);
  - `tutor` with `split` (Cultivate: `n: 2`, `split: [{n: 1, to: battlefield_tapped}, {n: 1, to: hand}]`) and `position` (Long-Term Plans: `to: library_top, position: 3`); `self_to_library` with `position` (Approach: seventh from the top);
  - `choose_on_enter {choice: creature_type | color}`; `type_grant` with `self: true` and `add_chosen_type: true` (Roaming Throne);
  - the event `play_land` ("whenever you play a land"; landfall is `enters` with a land subject);
  - the filter `controller: that_player` (the player the trigger or effect is about: Balefire Dragon's "each creature that player controls", with `damage.filter`), and `Target.for_each: opponent` (Grasp of Fate: "for each opponent, up to one target ... that player controls").
- **Lint.** The validator rejects what the schema can't: `detail` on a non-silent keyword, a tutor with both or neither of `to`/`split`, `position` off the library top, a `damage_dealt_this_turn` count without its `player`, a `type_grant` with both or neither of `filter`/`self`.
- **Status tables per field**, not just per construct: recursion to the library top/bottom, conditional enters-tapped (`unless`), land type grants (Urborg) and the new counts the engine doesn't have yet count as unread for `today`.

## Principles

1. **Say what the card does, in rules terms. Don't say how to pilot it.**
   - "Target player mills three cards" is `{"do": "mill", "n": 3, "who": "target_player"}`. Whether the pilot mills itself is the engine's decision.
   - The same goes for choosing numbers, picking modes, holding pumps and picking which blocker to kill.
2. **Never guess.**
   - Every object is closed: an unknown field, an unknown effect name or a wrong type is a rejection.
   - A line the format can't say is written as `unexpressible` with a reason.
   - A translator may never drop a line, approximate a count, loosen a filter or drop a condition to make something fit. (These are exactly the misread families the Phase 3 audit found in the regex parser.)
3. **Every Oracle line is accounted for.** Each ability carries the Oracle `text` it translates. The validator requires every line of the card (reminder text excluded) to be covered by some ability, and every ability's text to be a line of the card. Skipping a line, or inventing one, is a rejection.
4. **Expressible is not the same as executable.** GEF can describe more than goldfish.py runs today: coin flips, flicker, extra turns. Statuses are computed against the engine's real capability, so numbers stay comparable with the regex parser's.

## Card

```json
{"gef": "0.2", "name": "<Oracle name>", "abilities": [ ... ], "source": "llm", "notes": "..."}
```

- **`faces`.** Use it instead of `abilities` for cards whose faces each have text: MDFCs, split cards, adventures, transforming cards. It is a list of `{"name", "abilities"}`.
- **`pt`.** Printed power and toughness come from the card data; GEF doesn't repeat them. For `*` bodies, use the static `pt_equals`.
- **`source`.** One of `llm`, `hand`, `override` or `parser_export`.

## Abilities (`kind`)

Every ability has `text`: the Oracle line it translates, copied as written. The card name may be written as-is or as "~".

| kind | Use for | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| `keyword` | a keyword on its own line or in a keyword list ("Flying, vigilance" = two abilities) | `keyword` (from the list in the schema), `n` (toxic 2), `cost` (equip, cycling, kicker, evoke, flashback...), `card_filter` (what typecycling/landcycling finds), `filter` (equip's or offering's restriction), `detail` (silent keywords only: protection's quality, enchant's object) |
| `spell` | what an instant or sorcery does as it resolves | `effects` |
| `triggered` | when / whenever / at the beginning of | `event`, `if` (intervening "if"), `effects`, `optional` ("you may"), `once_per_turn` |
| `activated` | "cost: effect" | `cost`, `effects`, `sorcery_speed`, `once_per_turn`, `if` ("activate only if"), `from_zone` (graveyard, hand) |
| `mana` | a mana ability (it adds mana and does nothing else) | `cost`, `produce`, `if` |
| `static` | a continuous effect | `effect` (a Static), `if` ("as long as") |
| `loyalty` | planeswalker loyalty abilities | `cost` (+1, -3, "+X", "-X"), `effects` |
| `chapter` | Saga chapters | `chapters` ([1] or [1, 2]), `effects` |
| `class_level` | a Class's "{cost}: Level N" and the lines under it | `level`, `cost`, `abilities` (that level's lines) |
| `additional_cost` | "As an additional cost to cast this spell, ..." | `cost`, `optional` |
| `alt_cost` | "You may [pay X] rather than pay this spell's mana cost" | `cost`, `if` |
| `self_cost_reduction` | "This spell costs {1} less to cast for each ..." | `amount`, `if` |
| `unexpressible` | a whole line GEF can't say | `reason`, `scope` (below) |

## Effects (`do`)

Effects run in order; nested lists (`then`, `else`, `effects`, `modes`, `on_win`...) are effects too.

**Card flow**
- `draw {n, who}`
- `discard {n | "hand", who, random, filter}`
- `mill {n | "half_library", who}`
- `scry {n}`, `surveil {n}`
- `look {n, take, filter, to, rest, reveal, from}`: look at / reveal the top N, take some. `rest` is where the others go.
- `reveal_until {filter, then, rest}`: cascade-style.
- `tutor {filter, n, to, from, reveal, split, position}`: search. `from` can include the graveyard for "library and/or graveyard". `split` instead of `to` when the found cards go to different zones (Cultivate); `position` with `to: library_top` for "Nth from the top".
- `recur {filter, n | "all", to, from}`: from a graveyard (or exile) to a zone.
- `wheel {n, who, shuffle_graveyard}`: discard the hand and draw N.
- `put_back {n, to}`: Brainstorm-style.
- `impulse {n, until, filter, free}`: exile from the top; you may play them.
- `cast_free {filter, from, n}`: cast a card without paying.
- `free_cast_permission {spells, from, duration}`: cast spells without paying for a while.
- `put_from_hand {filter, n | "any", symmetric, until}`: Sneak Attack, Show and Tell (`symmetric`).

**Mana and lands**
- `mana {produce}`: a ritual.
- `extra_land {n}`, `land_from_hand {n, tapped}`
- `untap {what}`

**Board**
- `token {n, token, tapped, attacking, for_each_player, at_end_step}`
- `copy_token {of, n, haste, nonlegendary, at_end_step}`
- `counters {kind, n, on, remove}`, `proliferate {n}`
- `pump {target, power, toughness, keywords, duration}`
- `pump_team {filter, power, toughness, keywords, duration}`
- `sacrifice {what, who}`
- `bounce_own {what}`: return your own permanent to hand.
- `flicker {what, returns, tapped}`
- `animate {what, power, toughness, types, subtypes, keywords, duration}`
- `attach {what, to}`
- `grant {what, abilities, duration}`: a permanent gains abilities (Urza's Saga's chapters).
- `level {to}`
- `self_to_library {where, position}`, `bounce_self`

**Players and life**
- `damage {n, to, divided, source, filter}` (`filter` narrows `each_creature`), `lose_life {n, who}`, `gain_life {n, who}`, `set_life {n, who}`, `poison {n, who}`
- `win_game`, `lose_game {who}`
- `extra_turn {n}`, `extra_combat {n, untap, then_main}`
- `skip {what, who}`
- `monarch`, `initiative`, `venture`

**Interaction.** These are held by the engine or used against `--blockers`, and are out of scope in a clean goldfish game.
- `remove {how, target, amount, controller_compensation, duration}`. `how` is one of destroy, exile, bounce, tuck, damage, minus, edict, tap, cant_block, cant_attack, cant_attack_or_block.
- `wipe {how, filter, amount, one_sided}`
- `counter_spell {filter, unless_pays, abilities}`
- `protect {target, grant, duration}`
- `prevent_damage {text}`
- `gain_control {target, duration}`
- `opponent_discards {n, who, chooser, random}`

**Structure**
- `if {cond, then, else}`
- `may_pay {cost, then, else, who}`: "[you may] COST. If you do, ... Otherwise, ...".
- `choose {n, up_to, same_mode_twice, modes}`: "Choose one/two —".
- `flip_coins {n, until_lose, on_win, on_lose}`, `roll_die {sides, table}`, `choose_number {min, max}`
- `delayed {when, event, effects}`: "at the beginning of the next end step", "this turn, whenever ...".
- `copy_spell {of, n, filter}`
- `unless_opponent_pays {cost, effects}`: Rhystic taxes.
- `unexpressible {text, reason, scope}`: part of a line GEF can't say.

## Building blocks

- **Amount.** An integer, `"X"` (the spell's or ability's X), or a count:
  - Form: `{"count": KEY, "filter": PermFilter, "card_filter": CardFilter, "colors", "kind", "times", "plus", "divide", "round", "max"}`.
  - Keys include `permanents_you_control` (with a filter), `permanents_on_battlefield` (every player's, with a filter), `total_power` (of creatures you control matching the filter; attacking creatures are yours on your turn), `cards_in_hand`, `cards_in_graveyard`, `devotion`, `domain`, `converge`, `times_kicked`, `opponents`, `your_life_total`, `power_of_self`, `greatest_power`, `counters_on_self`, `spells_cast_this_turn`, `cards_drawn_this_turn`, `damage_dealt_this_turn` (to `player`: `target_opponent`, or `an_opponent` for "an opponent was dealt"), `commander_identity_colors`, `that_much`, `number_chosen`, `coin_flips_won`.
  - "Twice the number of" is `times: 2`; "1 plus" is `plus: 1`; "half, rounded down" is `divide: 2, round: down`.
  - If no key fits, the line is unexpressible. Never count something else instead.
- **PermFilter.** A permanent filter:
  - `types` (any of them, or all of them with `all_types: true`), `non_types`, `subtypes`, `non_subtypes`, `supertypes`, `colors`, `colorless`;
  - `controller` (you / opponent / any / that_player: the player the trigger or effect is about), `another`, `token` / `nontoken`, `power` / `toughness` / `mana_value` ranges, `keywords`, `with_counters`;
  - `tapped`, `attacking`, `equipped`, `enchanted`, `modified`, `historic`, `commander`, `chosen_type` (the type chosen by `choose_on_enter`), `entered_this_turn`, `name`;
  - `keywords` lists hold KeywordItems (a keyword name, or `{keyword, detail}` for a silent one);
  - `any_of` for "an artifact or creature".
  - A qualifier the filter can't hold makes the line unexpressible. The filter is never widened.
- **CardFilter.** For searches, recursion and spells: the same idea for cards in zones, plus `mana_value` (`max` may be `"X"` or `"sacrificed_plus_1"`), `mana_cost_any_of` (Urza's Saga's "{0} or {1}"), `different_names`, `any`.
- **Target.** `{"ref": self | target | each | that | equipped | enchanted | attached | sacrificed | commander | attackers | it, "n", "up_to", "filter", "for_each"}`. `for_each: opponent` means n targets for each opponent, each among that player's permanents (`filter.controller: that_player`).
- **Event.** `{"on": EVENT` (incl. `play_land`: "whenever you play a land", which a fetched or put-onto-the-battlefield land doesn't trigger), "subject", "spell", "who", "whose", "alone", "min_attackers", "first_each_turn", "nth", "one_or_more", "min_damage", "level"}`. `subject` is self, equipped, enchanted, self_or_another or a PermFilter.
- **Cond.** Condition types:
  - `control {filter, min, max}`;
  - life and hand bounds, `graveyard_at_least`, `library_empty`;
  - `attacked_this_turn`, `gained_life_this_turn`, `creature_died_this_turn`, `spells_cast_this_turn`;
  - `your_turn`, `main_phase`;
  - `kicked`, `cast_from_hand`, `self_tapped`, `x_at_least`, `mana_spent` (adamant), `delirium`, `amount_at_least {amount, n}`;
  - `not` / `and` / `or`.
- **Cost.** `{mana, tap, untap, sacrifice, discard, pay_life, remove_counters, put_counters, exile_from_graveyard, exile_from_hand, exile_self, tap_untapped, return_to_hand, exert, energy, loyalty, mill, reveal}`.
- **Mana.** `{"units": ["C", "C"]}` for Sol Ring; `["WU"]` is W or U; `"any"`, `"any_one_color"`, `"commander_colors"`, `"chosen_color"`; `amount` for "X mana"; `restrict` for "spend this mana only ..."; `any_combination`.
- **Token.** A `preset` (treasure, clue, food, gold, blood, map, powerstone, shard, junk, ...) or a definition: `{name, types, subtypes, colors, power, toughness, keywords, abilities, legendary}`.
- **Static (`static`).**
  - Bonuses and grants: `anthem`, `attached_bonus`, `grant_abilities`, `type_grant`, `evasion`, `cant_attack`, `cant_block`.
  - Costs: `cost_reduction`, `cost_increase`, `free_cast`, `alt_cost_all`.
  - Lands, hands and mana: `extra_land`, `no_max_hand_size` / `max_hand_size`, `spend_mana_as_any`, `lands_tap_any`.
  - Doublers: `token_doubler`, `counter_doubler`, `trigger_doubler`, `mana_multiplier`, `damage_multiplier`.
  - Entering and the permanent itself: `enters_tapped {unless, unless_pay}`, `enters_with_counters`, `doesnt_untap`, `pt_equals`, `choose_on_enter {choice}` ("As ~ enters, choose a creature type / a color"), `type_grant {self, add_chosen_type}` ("~ is the chosen type in addition to its other types").
  - Replacement and rule changes: `skip_step {what, who}`, `extra_counters {n, kind, on}`, `graveyard_replacement {whose, to}`, `discard_to_top`, `spell_limit {n, spells, who}`, `draw_from_empty_library_wins`, `coin_flip_rule {rule}`.
  - Other: `play_from_top`.
  - There is no free-text static. A replacement or restriction none of these says is `unexpressible`.

## The escape: `unexpressible`

A whole ability (`"kind": "unexpressible"`) or part of one (`"do": "unexpressible"`), with a `reason` and a `scope`:

| scope | Meaning | Effect on the status |
|---|---|---|
| `out_of_scope` | Text a goldfish can't use: an opponent's turn, their hand, a vote, their choices, protection from attacks that never come. | Never a miss. A card whose only unread lines are out of scope is `modeled` (or `vacuum` if nothing else is left). This is today's "needs opponents" rule. |
| `format_gap` | Rules text that matters to your game but GEF has no construct for (an opponent's pick of your cards, restarting the game). | A miss: the card is `partial`, or `blank` if nothing else reads. |
| `unclear` | The translator isn't sure what the text does. | A miss. |

This is how the format stays honest: text GEF can't say becomes an unread line, exactly like an unmodeled line today.

**Which scope?** Ask what the text does to *your* game: your cards, mana, board, life, or the opponents' life totals.
- Only to opponents' spells, permanents, hands or choices, with nothing coming back to you: `out_of_scope`. Counterbalance ("counter that spell" on an opponent's cast), Invert Polarity (an opponent's spell), Archfiend of Ifnir's -1/-1 counters on opponents' creatures, Sink into Stupor's bounce of an opponent's spell or permanent, Delighted Halfling's "can't be countered" rider.
- It touches your game and no construct says it: `format_gap`.
- Before either, check the constructs. T2 marked these as gaps though the format could say them: Chaos Warp is `remove how: tuck` (the reveal is the owner's); Victimize is `may_pay {sacrifice}` then `recur`; Ponder is a `look` at three that puts them all back on top (`rest: top`), then `draw` (only the optional shuffle is a gap); Breaching Dragonstorm is `reveal_until` + `cast_free` with an `else` to hand; Blast-Furnace Hellkite's "creatures attacking your opponents" is `attacking: true` (your creatures only attack opponents); Boros Charm's "target player or planeswalker" is `target_player` (planeswalkers are opponent-side); Gemstone Caverns' plain `{T}: Add {C}` is a mana ability even though its luck-counter line is a gap.
- "Each player can't ..." and "each player's ..." include you: never `out_of_scope` (Rule of Law, Deafening Silence).
- Mark the smallest part: if one clause is a gap, translate the rest of the line and put `do: unexpressible` for that clause only.

## Statuses: to and from goldfish.py

`validate.py --status` derives the status twice:
- **expressed:** as if the engine ran every GEF construct. This measures the format.
- **today:** constructs goldfish.py doesn't execute yet count as unread. This is the number comparable with the regex parser's. The support tables are `ENGINE_EFFECTS`, `ENGINE_EVENTS`, `ENGINE_CONDS`, `ENGINE_STATICS` and `ENGINE_KEYWORDS` in `validate.py`, taken from docs/GOLDFISH.md.

| goldfish status | From GEF |
|---|---|
| `modeled` | Every ability is read (all its constructs are supported) or silent (ward, hexproof, partner...), or is `unexpressible` with scope `out_of_scope`. At least one ability is read. |
| `partial` | Some abilities read, at least one missed: `format_gap` / `unclear`, or an unsupported construct for `today`. |
| `blank` | Nothing reads and something is missed. |
| `held` | An instant or sorcery whose read effects are interaction (`counter_spell`, `protect`, `remove`, `wipe`, ...). goldfish.py holds these as answers, as it does today. |
| `vacuum` | Everything is out of scope (Mindslaver, Council's Judgment, a discard spell). |
| `land` / `land*` | A land with nothing missed, or with something missed. |
| `override` | The card has a hand-written entry (see below). It wins over the translation. |

Going back the other way, the parser's `keyword not modeled`, `unmodeled` and `unread part` notes all correspond to GEF lines that are missed for `today`. `needs opponents` corresponds to `out_of_scope`.

## Overrides and existing hand-written reads

`data/goldfish_overrides.json` holds 5 hand-written readings (Leyline Immersion, Sylvan Library, Mystic Remora, Fact or Fiction, Meren of Clan Nel Toth) in a small effect-string DSL.

**Coexistence.** Precedence is override > GEF translation > regex parser.
- A card with an override keeps it.
- A card with a validated translation uses it.
- Everything else falls back to the regex parser, so a partial rollout never loses a read that works today.

**Migration.**
- Each override becomes a GEF card with `"source": "override"`. Its `note` goes into `notes`.
- The DSL's effect strings map one to one onto GEF effects: `draw 2` → `draw`, `recur hand a creature card` → `recur`, `face 2` → `damage` or `lose_life`, and so on.
- The two special tokens `sylvan` and `bob` stay as named engine behaviors until GEF has a construct for them.

After migration, the override file becomes a directory of hand-verified GEF cards and the DSL retires.

The regex parser's own readings could also be exported to GEF (`"source": "parser_export"`) to seed or cross-check translations. T2 compares at the level of effect signatures instead of building that exporter.

## Validation

- `python3 translation/gef_schema.py` regenerates the schema.
- `python3 translation/validate.py FILE.json` accepts or rejects each translation:
  - schema errors are listed with their JSON path;
  - line coverage is checked against the Oracle text in `data/`;
  - when the `jsonschema` package is present, it cross-checks the built-in validator.

Seven malformed samples in the T1 check were all rejected: an unknown field, a bad color code, two skipped lines, an invented line, a misspelled effect, a string where an amount belongs, and a fake card name.

## Worked examples

All 18 are in `translation/examples/examples.json` and accepted. Their statuses are shown as expressed / today.

**Easy**

| Card | GEF | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Sol Ring | `mana {tap} -> units [C, C]` | modeled / modeled |
| Llanowar Elves | `mana {tap} -> [G]` | modeled / modeled |
| Command Tower | `mana {tap} -> [commander_colors]` | land / land |
| Rampant Growth | `spell: tutor {types: [land], supertypes: [basic]} to battlefield_tapped` | modeled / modeled |
| Counterspell | `spell: counter_spell` | held / held |

**Medium**

- **Mulldrifter.** `keyword flying` + `triggered enters(self): draw 2` + `keyword evoke {2}{U}`. Status modeled / **partial**: evoke doesn't execute today.
- **Krenko, Mob Boss.** `activated {tap}: token n={count: permanents_you_control, filter: {subtypes: [Goblin]}}` of a 1/1 red Goblin. Status modeled / modeled.
- **Skullclamp.** `static attached_bonus +1/-1` + `triggered dies(equipped): draw 2` + `keyword equip {1}`. Status modeled / modeled.
- **Rhystic Study.** `triggered opponent_casts, optional: unless_opponent_pays {1}: draw 1`. The ~opp tax rule stays the engine's. Status modeled / modeled.
- **Kiki-Jiki.** `copy_token of target nonlegendary creature you control, haste, at_end_step: sacrifice`. Status modeled / modeled.

**Hard**

- **Urza's Saga.** Three chapters:
  - I and II use `grant` to give the Saga a mana ability and a token-making ability. The token carries its own `pt_equals` static.
  - III tutors an artifact with `mana_cost_any_of: ["{0}", "{1}"]`, which is not "mana value 1 or less": a {U} artifact has mana value 1 but not that mana cost.
  - Status land / **land\***: a general `grant` isn't executed today (the parser special-cases this card).
- **Caretaker's Talent.** The base trigger (`enters`, subject `{token: true, controller: you}`, `one_or_more`, `once_per_turn`) plus two `class_level`s. Level 2 holds a `class_level`-event trigger making a token copy; level 3 holds a token anthem. Status modeled / modeled.
- **Yusri, Fortune's Flame.** `choose_number 1..5`, then `flip_coins n=number_chosen` (win: draw 1; lose: 2 damage to you), then `if amount_at_least(coin_flips_won, 5): free_cast_permission from hand until end of turn`. Which number to choose is the pilot's policy, not the card's text. Status modeled / **partial**: coin flips are the Yusri deck's biggest missing mechanic (T0).
- **Astral Slide.** `triggered cycle (each_player), optional: flicker target creature, returns next_end_step`. Status modeled / **blank**.

**Not representable**

- **Gifts Ungiven.** Unexpressible, `format_gap`: an opponent picks which two of the four you keep. A goldfish needs a stand-in ("they give you the two worst"), and GEF 0.1 deliberately has no opponent-choice construct. Encoding a stand-in inside the translation would be encoding pilot policy as card text. Status blank.
- **Mindslaver.** Unexpressible, `out_of_scope`: you control an opponent's turn, and opponents take no decisions in a goldfish. Status vacuum.
- **Council's Judgment.** Unexpressible, `out_of_scope`: the other players' votes decide what is exiled. Status vacuum.
- **Karn Liberated.** +4 is out of scope (an opponent loses a card from hand), −3 is `remove exile target permanent`, and −14 ("restart the game ... with those cards") is a `format_gap`. Status partial.

## Known limits of v0.2

- **Opponent choices** (Gifts Ungiven, Fact or Fiction, "an opponent chooses one —") have no construct. They're format gaps until the engine has a stand-in rule for them.
- **T2's gap list** (`docs/TRANSLATION_T2.md`): 0.2 took the cheap ones. Still gaps: per-creature P/T doubling, "creatures that attacked this turn", either-of-two events, "leaves without dying", a d20 result as an amount, imprint and other card memory, phasing, manifest, redirect, mana that persists through phases, opponent choices.
- **Replacement effects** outside the 0.2 list (Words of Worship, Rhox Faithmender, ...) are `unexpressible` until a construct exists for them.
- **Free strings left.** None of them makes a line count as read on its own, but none is checked either: mana `restrict` ("spend this mana only ..."), `prevent_damage.text` (interaction, out of scope), `opponent_state.text`, `remove.controller_compensation`, `evasion.rule`, `token_doubler.filter`, `mana_multiplier.sources`, `Amount.kind`, `Event.event_text`, counter `kind`. The adapter (T3 step 2) must refuse any value it doesn't know.
- **Timing and priority** (flash, split second, "any time you could cast an instant") are silent or out of scope, matching the engine's model.
- **One ability per Oracle line.** Paragraphs that pack two abilities are one entry with several effects.
- **The status tables in `validate.py` are my reading of the engine.** A later compiler would replace them with the truth.
