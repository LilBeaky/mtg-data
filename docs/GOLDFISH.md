# GOLDFISH — goldfish.py

Companion to `USE_INSTRUCTIONS.md` §6. Read this only when running `goldfish.py`. This doc is expected to change as the simulator is tested and iterated on.


Plays the deck alone thousands of times with a greedy pilot and reports development and card flow. **Scope: the deck in a vacuum.** Opponents are never simulated as decision-makers; disruption to your own game is (see Disruption). Use it for timing questions ("when is the commander out?", "when does the payoff land?"), color reliability, card-advantage reads, and comparing swaps. Stats Math stays the tool for pure draw odds.

```
python3 scripts/goldfish.py DECK [--turns 8] [--trials 2000] [--draw] [--seed 1]
    [--track "Label=REGEX"] [--variant "Label|Out=>In;Out=>In"] [--kill-commander T]
    [--order commander,track,ramp,draw,other] [--disruption auto|ladder|sample|off|"wipe@5;cmd@4"] [--bracket N] [--shuffles N] [--horizon N] [--ladder-max] [--disruption-trace N|S:R]
    [--cast-interaction] [--no-mulligan] [--explain] [--trace N] [--json]
```

- **Always run `--explain` first** on a new list. Each card is marked `modeled`, `partial` (some lines unread), `blank` (cast for its cost, does nothing), `held` (removal/counters/protection instants and sorceries, never cast proactively; counters and protection can answer disruption), `vacuum` (its text needs opponents, so it does nothing here: Tithe Taker, Archivist of Oghma), or `override`. `~opp` marks cards running on the fixed opponent approximations below. Tell the user which important cards are partial, blank, vacuum or ~opp before quoting numbers.
- `--track "Myojin=^Myojin of"` reports the first turn a card group is cast or put onto the battlefield (tutored or reanimated counts; a bare card name works too). Tracked cards get cast priority right after the commander, and tutors fetch them first.
- `--variant` runs a swapped build on the **same shuffles** and prints a side-by-side table. Use it for every cut-vs-add question. It's far less noisy than two separate runs. `--explain --variant ...` also lists the incoming cards.
- `--kill-commander T` removes the commander after turn T-1 in every clean game (recast with tax; a held answer can stop it). For a deliberate "what if" on the main report. The disruption section covers this sampled.
- `--trace N` prints a play-by-play of game N. Use it to audit the pilot whenever a number looks off.
- Speed: about 2,000 clean games plus the 2,000-game ladder per build in 4 to 8 seconds (half that with `--disruption off`).

**What the report means.** A `tutor priorities` header line means the pilot read `# key:` / `# package:` lines (see Tutors below). Development rows give P10/median/P90. P10 is the floor, which the user cares about most. "Mana" counts sources available at the start of the main phase, and "all colors" counts restricted mana (e.g. Plaza of Heroes' legendary-only colors) as available. "Extra cards" are cards put into hand beyond draw steps, counted net: a wheel counts cards drawn minus the hand it threw away, and looting or Brainstorm-style put-backs subtract what leaves your hand, so they show as card filtering rather than card advantage. "Stranded" counts spells you had the mana *amount* for but not the colors. "Discarded" is cleanup discard, a flood signal. "Extra cards by source" names the card-advantage engines. "Graveyard" is its size at end of turn; "recursion" counts cards back from the graveyard (to hand, to the battlefield, or cast from it), with "recursion by source" naming them; "cycled" counts cycling and typecycling. "Tutor targets" lists what tutors actually fetched: read it to check the pilot is tutoring the way the user would.

**Model and fixed conventions.**
- London mulligan with the free first mulligan (rule 103.5c). Keep 3–5 lands, or 2 lands plus a cheap ramp piece.
- **Opponent approximations (fixed, no flags).** Cards that only work off other players get a best approximation, tagged `~opp`: 3 other turns a round (a 4-player pod: "each upkeep/end step" triggers fire there); each opponent draws 1 and casts 1 spell a turn (40% creatures), a second 30% of the time (Kraum, Monologue Tax); taxes (Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, Esper Sentinel) are paid 75% of the time, so each lands about once a round; an opponent holds 4 cards (Windfall); an opponent's land count is their turn count ("if an opponent controls more lands than you": Land Tax, Knight of the White Orchid, Gift of Estates). Other "if an opponent has more X" conditions do nothing. Cumulative upkeep permanents are let go after 3 upkeeps. Never model what opponents choose: what they cast for synergy, their extra draws, reanimating from their graveyards.
- Summoning sickness, enters-tapped rules (fast/slow/check/reveal/shock/battlebond), fetches, bounce lands, filter lands and converters, restricted mana, colored-only mana, alt costs (Jodah, Fist of Suns), Omniscience-style free casting, cost reducers, extra land drops, rituals (cast only when they enable a spell), X-draw spells (X ≥ 2), counters with Hardened Scales/Doubling Season-style modifiers, proliferate, remove-a-counter draw abilities (keeps one divinity/indestructible counter), planeswalker loyalty, activated draw/proliferate/tutor abilities, triggers on cast/ETB/landfall/upkeep/draw step/end step/proliferate, rebound, and leylines are all modeled.
- **Tutors** read their target and destination with `tutors.py`'s parser, so the two tools agree (MV limits, power, subtypes, "or" alternatives, transmute's same-MV). Destinations: hand, top, battlefield (tapped or not), graveyard, castable exile; exile with no way to cast does nothing. A target with words the parser can't read leaves the ability unused; other approximations (⚠ in tutors.py) are used and shown with `~` in `--explain`. The pilot fetches, in order: a `# key:` card not yet in hand or play; the missing piece of a `# package:` (more when the rest is assembled); a tutor that can reach one of those; then its general sense of value. It fetches a land only when short, avoids a second copy of something it has, and marks down cards it can't cast soon. Graveyard tutors (Entomb, Buried Alive) are cast only with a payoff: recursion in hand, play or library, or a flashback-style card castable from there. They pick a reanimation target first. They are never counted as card advantage.
- **Graveyard.** Discards, resolved spells, sacrifices, surveil, mill, cycling and "reveal N, take one, rest into your graveyard" fill it. Recursion to hand or battlefield (Regrowth, Reanimate, Exhume, Sheoldred's upkeep, ETB returners, Animate Dead-style Auras) and casting from it (flashback, jump-start, retrace, escape, unearth, harmonize's base cost) are modeled. Flashback/jump-start/escape spells are exiled after; retrace goes back; unearth is exiled at end step. Reanimation onto the battlefield waits for a target of MV 4+ (anything from turn 6). Only your own graveyard exists.
- **Cycling family.** Cycling (mana or life), typecycling/landcycling and transmute are hand abilities, with "when you cycle ~" riders. The pilot landcycles for a missed land drop, transmutes toward a key/package card, and spends leftover mana at end of turn cycling dead cards (more freely with a cycle payoff on the battlefield). Cycling is filtering: net 0 extra cards.
- **Life** starts at 40. The pilot won't pay life below 20 (Griselbrand, life-cost cycling) and Reanimate-style life loss counts. Other life gain and loss isn't tracked.
- **Disruption: two modes.** `--disruption auto` (default) runs the **ladder** when the bracket is 2, 3 or 4 (`# bracket:` header or `--bracket`), otherwise the **sampled** read. Never guess a bracket to get the ladder; ask.
- **Ladder** (`data/goldfish_gradients.json`). Per shuffle (100 by default, `--shuffles`): 5 clean baselines, then 15 rungs of the bracket's interaction ladder, on the same library order. That's 2,000 games; about 4 seconds on a normal list.
  - **Baselines** keep the library order but reseed the in-game randomness: tutor reshuffles, `~opp` coin flips, random effects. Their spread is the **noise band**. Every rung is compared against the shuffle's baseline **mean**, and a Δ smaller than the band is noise.
  - **Rungs** go from one late wipe (rung 1) to playing through the bracket's best control deck (rung 15). Each rung is the previous one plus one change: an added event, one moved earlier, or one upgraded. Events are `SLOT=code@T`, and a slot keeps its ID when it moves or upgrades.
  - **Firing.** Events don't all fire. Each slot rolls one number per shuffle, **once**, and fires at a rung if the roll is under `weight(code) × intensity(rung)`. That probability never decreases for a slot, so a slot that fired at rung 10 fires at every higher rung on that shuffle. The ladder stays nested and each shuffle's curve can be compared. A `!` (backed up: your first answer gets countered) is its own roll. Expected events at rung 15 are about 5 (B2), 5.8 (B3) and 7.5 (B4). `--ladder-max` fires everything; use it as a stress test or to audit the pilot, not as the read.
  - **Horizon** is where the scripted interaction ends: B2 T10, B3 T9, B4 T8 (`--horizon`). Games stop there today. Once win/loss detection exists, games will run until a win or loss, and the horizon will only mark where the interaction script ends. A struggling deck is never cut short: the struggle is data.
  - **Codes:** `rem` (removal to graveyard; biggest permanent half the time), `rem+` (exile; key/tracked cards first, then highest MV), `cmd`, `wipe`, `nuke` (nonland wipe, Treasures too), `nuke+gy` (Farewell: exile nonland permanents and your graveyard), `rift` (your nonland permanents to hand, commander to the command zone; cleanup discard applies), `ctr4/ctr3/ctr2` (counter your first spell of that MV or more that turn, or your commander), `ctrK` (held from turn T until you cast your commander or a key/tracked card), `gy` (exile your graveyard), `ld` (destroy a land, nonbasic with the most colors first), `tax` (noncreature spells cost {1} more, Thalia), `taxall` (all spells, Sphere), `lock` (no casting from the command zone or graveyard; unearth still works; Drannith).
  - **Persistent effects.** `tax`/`taxall`/`lock` last 3 of your turns, or until the pilot kills the piece with held removal. Thalia and Drannith count as creatures and Sphere as an artifact. `--explain` shows "can kill tax/lock pieces" on held removal; that parse is approximate. Taxes also raise the cost of your held answers.
  - **Answers.** Counters answer every spell-based event, including counters, tax and lock pieces, but not `gy` (Bojuka Bog) or `ld` (Strip Mine). Protection answers removal, wipes and rift; against `nuke+gy` it saves the board, but the graveyard is still exiled. Redirects answer targeted removal.
  - **Fold.** A game folds when a hit event pushed it below the deck's clean floor (P10 of resolved spells across all baselines) from at or above it, or cost 2+ commander turns against the shuffle's baseline mean. Both thresholds are set in the gradients file. Shuffles already below the floor when clean are reported separately and fold only on commander turns.
  - **Reading it:**
    - The rung table shows fired/hit events, answered %, Δ resolved spells/mana/extra cards/board/recursion/commander turns/dead turns (turns with nothing cast), rebuilt (share of games whose first board hit got back to the pre-hit board size by the horizon, with median turns), and fold %.
    - Below it, the **breakpoint**: per shuffle, the first rung that folds (P25/median/P75, plus never-folded %).
    - **"What changed at the breakpoint"** counts the events that were new or moved at each shuffle's breakpoint. That is the diagnosis: which interaction this deck folds to.
    - Δspells counts resolved spells. Positive Δspells or Δmana after hits means rebuilding with spare mana (recasts, commander tax); read that as resilience, not gain.
    - Rebuild reads are cut off by the horizon, so a late hit can't show a recovery.
  - `--variant` adds ladder rows (median breakpoint, never folded, average and top-rung fold, top-rung Δcommander turns).
  - `--disruption-trace S:R` prints shuffle S at rung R.
- **Sampled** (`--disruption sample`; the fallback when there's no ladder bracket). Every game is replayed on the same shuffle with 0-2 events: 25% none, 50% one, 25% two, on turns 3+. Events are commander removal, spot removal, creature wipe, nonland wipe, and a counterspell on your first MV 3+ spell (or commander). The table reports the paired average change per scenario against the same clean game.
- **Fixed** (`--disruption "wipe@5;cmd@4;ctrK!@3"`, any ladder code): every game faces the same events. Pair it with `--variant` to compare builds against the same hit. `--disruption-trace N` shows game N; `--disruption off` skips disruption.
- **All modes:**
  - Removed cards go to the graveyard (exile for `rem+`/`nuke+gy`), so recursion can rebuild.
  - Held counters, protection and redirects answer an event if you left the mana open, or for free with a commander out (Fierce Guardianship).
  - The pilot never taps out at end of turn to cycle below its cheapest held answer, but it doesn't hold mana in the main phase.
  - "Answered" is how often your interaction saved you; "no target" is how often there was nothing to hit (a slow start dodges wipes).
  - Commander turns lost is usually the headline.
- **Not modeled:** combat, opponents' graveyards, token copies, noncreature tokens other than Treasure/Clue/Gold, copies, dredge, delve, disturb/embalm/eternalize/encore/aftermath, graveyard costs that aren't mana/discard/exile-from-graveyard (Dread Return's sacrifice), "for each card in your graveyard" counts, and anything the explain list marks partial or blank. Say so when these matter to the question.
- **Tokens.** Creature tokens enter as permanents with their power and subtypes (they count for board size, "greatest power", creature counts and enter triggers, and vanish when they leave). A quoted ability on the token is read like card text (Eldrazi Scion mana). Clues ({2}, sacrifice: draw) and Gold are modeled; investigate makes a Clue. "For each X" counts use the same keys as draw ("for each creature you control"); a count the sim can't see is skipped. Tokens made off an opponent's creature (Kalitas, Beast Within) are skipped. A cap of 150 permanents stops runaway loops.
- **Mana scaling.** Multipliers ("three times as much": Nyxbloom, Mana Reflection) and per-tap extras (Vorinclex, Zendikar Resurgent, Crypt Ghast) apply to every source they name; one entering mid-turn rescales untapped sources. Variable abilities read their count when tapped: greatest power (Selvala), "for each Elf" (Priest of Titania), creatures (Cradle), devotion.
- **Reducers and free casting.** Filters read card types, colors, real subtypes (from the CR 205.3 list: "Dragon spells"), power floors (Goreclaw), and "the first creature spell each turn" (Shadow in the Warp). "~ costs {1} less for each X" reads the same count keys. "Cast X spells without paying" takes a filter; without "from your hand" it covers the command zone too (Dracogenesis on a Dragon commander; tax still paid).
- **X-tutors** (Nature's Rhythm, Chord, GSZ, Finale) only find cards with mana value at most the X paid. The pilot pays all spare mana into X.
- **Modal spells** ("Choose one —") use the best goldfish mode: draw, then look/tutor/recur, land search, Treasure, tokens, mana. "One or both"/"two" take the top two.
- **Opponent landfall** ("whenever a land an opponent controls enters") is a fixed ~opp approximation: it fires on each opponent turn while their land count is still growing.
- Upkeep/end-step triggers with an intervening "if" (other than "if an opponent controls more lands") are left unmodeled and shown in `--explain`, not fired every turn.
- Conditional upgrades ("...instead if" threshold, kicker, delirium, addendum lines) are modeled at their **base** effect only and the upgrade line shows as unmodeled. Cabal Ritual makes BBB, not BBBBB.
- Permanents that grant extra land drops (Exploration, Azusa) give them the turn they enter.

## Fixing a card

**Fixing a card: `data/goldfish_overrides.json`.** Key = Oracle name. An entry replaces only the fields it names; always add a `note`. Fields: `mana` (list of `{"colors": "any" | "WU" | "C", "count": n, "restrict": "legendary", "colored_only": false, "sick": true}`), `etb`, `spell` (lists of effect strings), `triggers` (list of `{"on": "cast|etb|landfall|upkeep|end|drawstep|prolif|cycle|opp_cast|opp_draw|opp_second", "filter": "noncreature", "do": [...], "once": false, "tax": false, "each": false}`), `activated` (list of `{"cost": "{2}", "tap": true, "sac": false, "remove": "divinity 1", "do": [...]}`), `hold`, `requires`, `cat`, `skip`, `status`. Effect strings: `draw 2`, `draw permanents` (also lands, creatures, artifacts, power, colors, opp_hand), `scry 2`, `surveil 1`, `look 3 1`, `prolif 1`, `treasure 1`, `extra_land 1`, `land_from_hand 1`, `land basic bf_t 1`, `ctr divinity 1`, `mana WUBRG`, `tutor DEST TARGET` and `recur DEST TARGET` (DEST `hand`/`top`/`bf`/`graveyard`; TARGET in Oracle words, e.g. `recur hand a creature card`), `mill 3`. When a parse miss affects many cards, fix the parser in `goldfish.py` instead of piling up overrides.
