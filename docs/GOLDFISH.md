# GOLDFISH — goldfish.py

Companion to `USE_INSTRUCTIONS.md` §6. Read this only when running `goldfish.py`. This doc is expected to change as the simulator is tested and iterated on.


Plays the deck alone thousands of times with a greedy pilot and reports development, card flow and combat. **Scope: the deck in a vacuum.** Opponents are never simulated as decision-makers: they are three life totals your deck attacks and drains (see Combat), with the creatures and combat denial `--blockers` gives them (see Blockers), and no attacks back. Disruption to your own game is simulated (see Disruption). Use it for timing questions ("when is the commander out?", "when does the payoff land?"), color reliability, card-advantage reads, the deck's clock (damage by turn, when the table dies), and comparing swaps. Stats Math stays the tool for pure draw odds.

```
python3 scripts/goldfish.py DECK [--turns 8] [--trials 2000] [--draw] [--seed 1]
    [--track "Label=REGEX"] [--variant "Label|Out=>In;Out=>In"] [--kill-commander T]
    [--order commander,track,ramp,draw,other] [--disruption auto|ladder|sample|off|"wipe@5;cmd@4"] [--bracket N] [--shuffles N] [--horizon N] [--ladder-max] [--disruption-trace N|S:R]
    [--blockers "SPEC"] [--cast-interaction] [--no-mulligan] [--explain] [--trace N] [--json]
```

- **Always run `--explain` first** on a new list. Each card is marked `modeled`, `partial` (some lines unread), `blank` (none of its text read: cast for its cost; a creature still attacks with its printed body), `held` (removal/counters/protection instants and sorceries, never cast proactively; counters and protection can answer disruption), `vacuum` (its text needs opponents, so it does nothing here: Tithe Taker, Archivist of Oghma, Mind Rot, Propaganda; a card whose only unread lines are opponent-only, like a discard rider, counts as `modeled` and still shows the "needs opponents" line. A line that touches you, their life totals or their creatures is never opponent-only: Rhystic-style "you draw", drains, edicts and taps stay real misses), or `override`. Two notes keep `modeled` honest: `keyword not modeled: kicker` (a keyword line nothing reads; ward, partner, enchant, landwalk and the like are silent) and `unread part: ...` (a line whose effect was read with a clause left over: Frantic Search's untap, a condition like "if X is 10 or more"); either makes the card `partial`. `~opp` marks cards running on the fixed opponent approximations below. Creatures end their line with P/T and combat keywords (`X/X (X = creatures)` for counted bodies); Equipment/Auras show their bonus and equip cost. Tell the user which important cards are partial, blank, vacuum or ~opp before quoting numbers.
- `--track "Myojin=^Myojin of"` reports the first turn a card group is cast or put onto the battlefield (tutored or reanimated counts; a bare card name works too). Tracked cards get cast priority right after the commander, and tutors fetch them first.
- `--variant` runs a swapped build on the **same shuffles** and prints a side-by-side table. Use it for every cut-vs-add question. It's far less noisy than two separate runs. `--explain --variant ...` also lists the incoming cards.
- `--kill-commander T` removes the commander after turn T-1 in every clean game (recast with tax; a held answer can stop it). For a deliberate "what if" on the main report. The disruption section covers this sampled.
- `--trace N` prints a play-by-play of game N. Use it to audit the pilot whenever a number looks off.
- Speed: about 2,000 clean games plus the 2,000-game ladder per build in 5 to 10 seconds (half that with `--disruption off`); go-wide token decks that hit the 150-permanent cap run up to twice that.

**What the report means.** A `tutor priorities` header line means the pilot read `# key:` / `# package:` lines (see Tutors below). Development rows give P10/median/P90. P10 is the floor, which the user cares about most. "Mana" counts sources available at the start of the main phase, and "all colors" counts restricted mana (e.g. Plaza of Heroes' legendary-only colors) as available. "Extra cards" are cards put into hand beyond draw steps, counted net: a wheel counts cards drawn minus the hand it threw away, and looting or Brainstorm-style put-backs subtract what leaves your hand, so they show as card filtering rather than card advantage. "Stranded" counts spells you had the mana *amount* for but not the colors. "Discarded" is cleanup discard, a flood signal. "Extra cards by source" names the card-advantage engines. "Graveyard" is its size at end of turn; "recursion" counts cards back from the graveyard (to hand, to the battlefield, or cast from it), with "recursion by source" naming them; "cycled" counts cycling and typecycling. "Tutor targets" lists what tutors actually fetched: read it to check the pilot is tutoring the way the user would. The **combat and damage** section is cumulative at end of turn: attackers that turn, combat damage, all damage to opponents (combat plus drains/burn), the most combat damage one commander has dealt one opponent, the most poison on one opponent, opponents dead (mean) and your life (median). Below it: when the first opponent and the whole table died (by turn), how kills happened (combat, noncombat, commander damage, poison), damage by source, **triggers fired by kind** (enter, cast, timed = upkeep/end/draw step/beginning of combat, combat, dies, ~opp, other) and the top trigger sources. A game ends when all three opponents are dead; its later turns repeat its final state, so later-turn development rows include finished games.

**Model and fixed conventions.**
- London mulligan with the free first mulligan (rule 103.5c). Keep 3–5 lands, or 2 lands plus a cheap ramp piece or three spells of MV 2 or less.
- **Opponent approximations (fixed, no flags).** Cards that only work off other players get a best approximation, tagged `~opp`: one other turn per living opponent each round (a 4-player pod; "each upkeep/end step" triggers fire there, so they slow down as opponents die; the mana you left open at the end of your turn is still there, see "Sacrifice costs and the untap engine"); each opponent draws 1 and casts 1 spell a turn (40% creatures), a second 30% of the time (Kraum, Monologue Tax); taxes (Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, Esper Sentinel) are paid 75% of the time, so each lands about once a round; an opponent holds 4 cards (Windfall); an opponent's land count is their turn count ("if an opponent controls more lands than you": Land Tax, Knight of the White Orchid, Gift of Estates). Other "if an opponent has more X" conditions do nothing. Cumulative upkeep permanents are let go after 3 upkeeps. Never model what opponents choose: what they cast for synergy, their extra draws, reanimating from their graveyards.
- Summoning sickness, enters-tapped rules (fast/slow/check/reveal/shock/battlebond), fetches, bounce lands, filter lands and converters, restricted mana, colored-only mana, alt costs (Jodah, Fist of Suns), Omniscience-style free casting, cost reducers, extra land drops, rituals (cast only when they enable a spell), X-draw spells (X ≥ 2), counters with Hardened Scales/Doubling Season-style modifiers, proliferate, remove-a-counter draw abilities (keeps one divinity/indestructible counter), planeswalker loyalty, activated draw/proliferate/tutor abilities, triggers on cast/ETB/landfall/upkeep/draw step/end step/proliferate, rebound, and leylines are all modeled.
- **More mana and cost reads.** "{T}: Untap target land/Forest" (Arbor Elf, Voyaging Satyr, Krosan Restorer) is a mana source that makes the best matching land's mana again. "Aura spells you cast that target enchanted creature cost {3} less" and "equip abilities ... cost {3} less" (Strong Back) are read as always on (~approx: Auras go on the creature it enchants). "Enchantment spells you cast have affinity for Auras" (Pearl-Ear) reduces generic by the Auras you control.
- **Cast triggers that need a target.** "Whenever you cast a spell that targets a creature you control / a modified permanent / ~" (Season of Growth, Pearl-Ear, heroic) fire for creature Auras, "target creature" pumps and counters, and protection spells, aimed at your best attacker (~approx). Constellation-style "whenever ~ or another enchantment enters" fires on itself too.
- **Tutors** read their target and destination with `tutors.py`'s parser, so the two tools agree (MV limits, power, subtypes, "or" alternatives, transmute's same-MV). Destinations: hand, top, battlefield (tapped or not), graveyard, castable exile; exile with no way to cast does nothing. A target with words the parser can't read leaves the ability unused; other approximations (⚠ in tutors.py) are used and shown with `~` in `--explain`. The pilot fetches, in order: a `# key:` card not yet in hand or play; the missing piece of a `# package:` (more when the rest is assembled); a tutor that can reach one of those; then its general sense of value. It fetches a land only when short, avoids a second copy of something it has, and marks down cards it can't cast soon. Graveyard tutors (Entomb, Buried Alive) are cast only with a payoff: recursion in hand, play or library, or a flashback-style card castable from there. They pick a reanimation target first. They are never counted as card advantage.
- **Graveyard.** Discards, resolved spells, sacrifices, surveil, mill, cycling and "reveal N, take one, rest into your graveyard" fill it. Recursion to hand or battlefield (Regrowth, Reanimate, Exhume, Sheoldred's upkeep, ETB returners, Animate Dead-style Auras) and casting from it (flashback, jump-start, retrace, escape, unearth, harmonize's base cost) are modeled. Flashback/jump-start/escape spells are exiled after; retrace goes back; unearth is exiled at end step. Reanimation onto the battlefield waits for a target of MV 4+ (anything from turn 6). Only your own graveyard exists.
- **Cycling family.** Cycling (mana or life), typecycling/landcycling and transmute are hand abilities, with "when you cycle ~" riders. The pilot landcycles for a missed land drop, transmutes toward a key/package card, and spends leftover mana at end of turn cycling dead cards (more freely with a cycle payoff on the battlefield). Cycling is filtering: net 0 extra cards.
- **Life** starts at 40 and only the deck lowers it: opponents never attack or drain you, so the only life lost is what you pay yourself. The floor is set by bracket (`FLOOR_BY_BRACKET`): 10 for Bracket 4, 20 otherwise (and with no bracket). The pilot never makes an *optional* payment that would take it below the floor. Paid and tracked, by kind in the report (`life you paid yourself`): mana pain (painlands only for colored mana, their {C} is free; City of Brass, Mana Confluence, Ancient Tomb, Horizon lands, Talismans; painless sources are tapped first, and a painful source that would kill you is never tapped), shocklands (pay 2 for untapped while at or above 20 after paying, else enter tapped), fetchland life, Phyrexian mana on spells (2 life each above the floor, otherwise that colored pip is paid with mana) and on activated abilities, "as an additional cost, pay N life", "pay N life" activations and cycling, "you lose N life" (including "draw a card and lose 1 life"), Dark Confidant (loses the revealed card's MV), Sylvan Library (keeps one extra card for 4 life per turn while above the floor), and your share of "each player loses" drains. Forced losses (Confidant, drains) can go below the floor; at 0 you die and the game ends there as a loss. **Decking** is the other loss: a draw from an empty library loses at once unless a Laboratory Maniac effect (Lab Man, Jace, Wielder of Mysteries) is on the battlefield, which wins instead. Thassa's Oracle is held until your devotion to blue (counting its own pip) covers the library, then wins as it enters. The report's `you lost` line gives the loss share, median turn, and the split between life payments and decking. The combat table's "your life" column is P10/median/P90. Not yet: Necropotence, Bolas's Citadel, Ad Nauseam, Channel's life, X-life costs (Toxic Deluge), alternate life costs (Snuff Out, Force of Will).  Reanimate-style life loss, "you gain/lose N life", lifelink and "each player loses" drains are tracked; opponents never attack you, so nothing else lowers it. Life gained is counted per turn (yours and each opponent's) for "if you gained life this turn", and "whenever you gain life" triggers fire once per gain event (Well of Lost Dreams pays X up to the life gained, holding the untap engine's mana; Heliod; Trudge Garden). Lifelink covers noncombat damage too: a lifelinked Ragost dealing 3 to each opponent gains 9 in one event. "Whenever enchanted creature / ~ deals damage, you gain that much life" (Spirit Loop) fires on combat and noncombat damage. Life loss that isn't damage ("each opponent loses 3 life") never triggers lifelink.
- **Combat.** Every turn runs main phase 1 (the pilot casts everything, since there is nothing to hide), equips, then combat, then main phase 2 (activations, plus anything combat drew or made).
  - **Opponents** are three players at 40 life. One dies at 0 life, 10 poison, or 21 combat damage from one commander (tallied per commander, per opponent). Killing all three ends the game. They never attack. Their creatures and combat denial come from `--blockers` (see Blockers); without it every attack is unblocked and the clock is a ceiling. Say which when quoting it. The blocker gradient (boards scaled by turn and bracket, like the interaction ladder) will feed the same channel.
  - **Attackers.** Every untapped creature that isn't summoning sick attacks (haste counts, including granted haste). The exceptions stay home: power 0 without an attack payoff, defender or "can't attack", a Theros god below its devotion, and a creature with a card-flow tap ability (it keeps the tap unless it has vigilance). The pilot taps lands and rocks for mana before would-be attackers.
  - **Targets.** Each attacker, biggest first, goes at an opponent it finishes off this combat; otherwise at the opponent closest to dying (the nearest of life, poison and commander damage). That is focus fire: the same opponent keeps taking the commander's damage. A player who dies to attack triggers takes their attackers out of combat.
  - **Damage.** First-strike and regular damage steps, double strike, trample, deathtouch, lifelink, indestructible, infect (poison instead of life loss), toxic/poisonous, and myriad (approximated: the same damage to each other opponent, no commander damage). Commander damage counts combat damage only. **Damage doublers** (Furnace of Rath, Dictate of the Twin Gods: x2; City on Fire, Fiery Emancipation: x3) multiply combat damage (after assignment, so commander damage and infect poison double too) and noncombat damage, never life loss; several multiply together. Every modeled source is yours, so Furnace's "any source" and City's "a source you control" read the same; Furnace doubling damage to you only shows on "each player" damage.
  - **Blocks**: see Blockers below.
  - **Keywords read:** flying, reach, trample, vigilance, haste, lifelink, deathtouch, menace, first strike, double strike, indestructible, defender, infect, wither, shadow, horsemanship, fear, intimidate, skulk, prowess, exalted (each instance, including granted), battle cry, melee, dethrone, training, myriad, living weapon, toxic/poisonous N, "can't be blocked", and the blocker keywords flanking, bushido, rampage and afflict (see Blockers). Annihilator and mentor are read but do nothing.
  - **Power and toughness** are the printed P/T, plus counted bodies ("~'s power and toughness are each equal to the number of creatures you control", your life total, cards in hand), +1/+1 and -1/-1 counters, anthems ("other Elves you control get +1/+1", "creature tokens you control get +1/+1 and have vigilance", "~ gets +1/+1 for each artifact you control", "creatures you control have haste"), Equipment and Auras on the creature, and until-end-of-turn pumps. Qualified counts ("for each other attacking Goblin") are left unread and show as partial.
  - **Equipment and Auras.** Before combat the pilot pays equip costs to put unattached Equipment on its best attacker: evasive first, then one with a combat-damage payoff, then the commander, then the most power. Every creature Aura goes on the best attacker as it enters, before its ETB (Sage's Reverie counts itself); Animate Dead goes on the creature it returned. Removal Auras ("loses all abilities", "can't attack", "base power and toughness", Kenrith's Transformation, Darksteel Mutation) and debuff Auras never go on your own creature. Conditional bonuses are read: "as long as enchanted creature is green" (Shield of the Oversoul) and "as long as another Aura is attached" (Face of Divinity); so are "+N/+N for each Aura (and Equipment) attached to it" (Strong Back, Kor Spiritdancer) and "for each Aura you control that's attached to a creature" (Sage's Reverie). Equipment stays when its creature leaves; Auras go to the graveyard.
  - **Additional combat phases.** "There is an additional combat phase" is read on spells (Relentless Assault), triggers (Aurelia and Karlach, first combat only; Port Razer; Moraug's landfall) and combat-only activated abilities (Najeela's, used after combat damage when the mana is there). Each additional combat follows its own main phase and reruns everything: beginning-of-combat, attack and combat-damage triggers fire again, and tokens that entered attacking can attack. Only untapped creatures attack again. "Untap all creatures you control / that attacked this turn / attacking creatures" riders untap them; one cast before the first combat waits for the extra combat. At most 5 additional combats per turn (Port Razer's once-per-opponent limit isn't read). Extra-combat spells are held like pumps: cast when the second attack kills an opponent or is worth 2 x MV + 2 damage. The report gives additional combat phases per game.
  - **Granted abilities.** Quoted abilities that a card gives are read as that card's triggers: "Enchanted/Equipped creature has \"Whenever this creature attacks / deals combat damage to a player, ...\"" (Mark of Sakiko, Snake Umbra, Bear Umbra), "Commander creatures you own have \"...\"" (Backgrounds such as Flaming Fist; commander anthems too), "Creatures you control have \"{T}: Add ...\"" (Cryptolith Rite: each unsick creature without its own mana ability taps for mana) and "Enchanted land has \"{T}: Add two mana ...\"" (Gift of Paradise, read as +1 mana; a one-mana grant like Abundant Growth is fixing only). "Add that much {G}" adds the combat damage just dealt; "untap all lands you control" gives the lands' mana again for main phase 2.
  - **Attack limits and base P/T.** "No more than one creature can attack each combat" (Silent Arbiter) limits your attack too: the pilot sends the hardest hitter. "Other creatures have base power and toughness 2/2" (Kudo) replaces the base of your other creatures before counters, anthems and Auras.
  - **Pumps and alpha strikes** (Giant Growth, Overrun, Craterhoof) are held for main phase 1 of a turn where they kill an opponent, or add at least 2 x their mana value + 2 damage.
- **Blockers (`--blockers "SPEC"`).** Gives opponents creatures that block and combat denial, for every game in the run (clean games, ladder baselines and rungs alike). Tokens split by `;`:
  - **Creature:** `[SEAT:]P/T[ keyword...]@T[-U][xN]`. SEAT is 1-3 or `each` (default each opponent). `@T`: on that board for your turn-T combat (it arrives on their turn before); `-U`: gone after your turn U; `xN`: N copies. Keywords: flying, reach, deathtouch, first strike, double strike, indestructible, lifelink, infect, wither, flanking, horsemanship, shadow, the five colors, artifact, wall (two-word ones with a space or `_`). Example: `1/1@2; 2/2 flying@4x2; 1:3/3 deathtouch@6`.
  - **Denial:** `[SEAT:]CODE@T[-U]` (default seat 1): `fog` (the first attack at that opponent from T on is fogged), `settle` (Settle the Wreckage: when two or more creatures or a lethal attack come at it, every attacker is exiled and you fetch that many tapped basics), `prop` (Propaganda / Ghostly Prison: {2} per creature attacking that opponent, paid from the mana left after main phase 1, else attackers go elsewhere), `arb` (Silent Arbiter: a 1/5 artifact blocker; one attacker per combat while it lives), `bridge` (Ensnaring Bridge: power greater than your hand size can't attack), `moat` (non-flyers can't attack), `maze` (Maze of Ith: once a round, your biggest attacker at that opponent is untapped and deals and takes no combat damage). Propaganda, Arbiter, Bridge and Moat are cast on the opponent's turn, so a held counter with open mana can stop them; fog and Settle are countered in your combat. A dead opponent's board and denial leave with them. Bad tokens exit with the syntax.
  - **Blocking rules:** flying/reach, shadow, horsemanship, fear, intimidate (shares a color), skulk, menace (two blockers), "can't be blocked", and partial evasion read from text: "by creatures with power N or less / N or greater / greater power", "by more than one creature", "except by three or more", "by / except by" colors, artifacts, walls, flyers (`--explain` shows them). Unread partial evasion blocks as normal and is noted.
  - **The defenders' policy** (deterministic, `plan_blocks`): per attacker, biggest first, one blocker that kills it and/or survives it (first strike, double strike, deathtouch, indestructible, flanking and the attacker's bushido counted); else a gang block of two that kill it losing at most one (or both survive); else, only when the attack would kill that player (life, commander damage, poison), the cheapest chump. Blockers used once per combat.
  - **The pilot** reads that same policy on its whole attack before committing: an attacker that would die without taking a blocker with it is sent at another opponent, or held back if every opponent would do that; the commander is never traded away (unless indestructible). Because blockers run out, a wide attack still gets its extra attackers through. Tokens entering attacking weren't planned, so they can die to blocks.
  - **After blocks:** bushido and rampage pump, flanking gives each non-flanking blocker -1/-1 (a 1-toughness blocker dies at once and the attacker stays blocked), afflict makes the defender lose N, "whenever ~ / a creature you control becomes blocked" triggers fire. Blocker damage: deathtouch kills, infect/wither gives -1/-1 counters, lifelink gains the opponent life.
  - **Your answers:** held removal (the kind that clears stax) is cast before combat on a Silent Arbiter, Moat or Bridge holding attackers home, on Propaganda held by the opponent you're killing, or on the one blocker between your attack and killing an opponent (only a spell that can kill it: Doom Blade skips a black blocker, Shock needs toughness 2 or less, destroy and damage skip indestructible, an edict takes their weakest creature; Swords' life gain counts against the kill). A one-sided wipe (Plague Wind) goes when clearing every blocker is lethal. Otherwise held removal stays held for disruption: it's never fired just to trade.
  - **Removal on permanents** (Ravenous Chupacabra, Nekrataal, Skinrender's -1/-1 counters, O-Ring effects): read as `removal:` in `--explain` and aimed at opponents' blockers, never your own board. An ETB or trigger hits the blocker whose removal lets the most damage through at the opponent you're killing, else the biggest legal one; with no `--blockers` it does nothing. Repeatable activated removal (not sacrifice abilities) is used in main phase 2 when there's a legal target. Bounced blockers come back on their controller's next turn. Unread on purpose: graveyard targets, flicker (exile then return), qualified targets ("attacking", "tapped", "it's blocking", "with a counter"), removal that hands them a body (Beast Within, Unwanted Remake) and counts ("for each"). "Can't block this turn" (all, non-flyers as Falter, or N target creatures: attack triggers pick the defending player's best blockers) and "can't be blocked this turn" spells are held like pumps and valued by the damage the blockers would stop. An opponent's `wipe`/`nuke`/`nuke+gy` disruption event kills their creatures too (not indestructible ones, except to Farewell), and a nonland wipe takes Propaganda, Bridge and Moat; `rift` leaves their boards (the caster's side isn't modeled).
  - **Report:** an `opp blockers` column (creatures on living opponents' boards at your combat), a blocks line (attackers blocked: chumped / traded / lost to the blocker / stalled; attackers held back from a bad block; blockers killed; combat damage stopped by blocks, fog and Maze; your removal on a blocker) and a denial line (fogs, Settles and creatures exiled, attackers kept home by Moat/Bridge/Arbiter/tax, tax paid, Maze, countered, your removal on denial).
- **Triggers** are read and counted by kind in the report:
  - cast (including prowess); enter (the card's own ETB and "whenever a creature enters"); landfall;
  - timed (upkeep, end step, draw step, beginning of combat);
  - combat (attacks, attacks alone, "whenever you attack", "attacks and isn't blocked", "becomes blocked", combat damage to a player, "one or more creatures deal combat damage", equipped/enchanted creature triggers);
  - dies ("when ~ dies", "whenever a creature you control dies", "~ or another creature"), and a permanent's own "when ~ is put into a graveyard from the battlefield" / "enters or is put into a graveyard" (Prized Statue, Ichor Wellspring, Servo Schematic; Spirit Loop and Rancor return to hand) and "when ~ leaves the battlefield" (Munitions), tokens included;
  - ~opp; other: cycle, proliferate, sacrifice ("whenever you sacrifice a Food / an artifact or creature": Nuka-Cola, Experimental Confectioner, Tireless Tracker; "whenever a player sacrifices" reads your sacrifices only), life gain, and "enchanted creature deals damage".

  Creatures that die to disruption, combat or a sacrifice fire dies triggers. Every sacrifice (a cost, a Treasure or Clue cashed in, Sakura-Tribe Elder) fires sacrifice triggers with the types the permanent had, granted ones included. Intervening-"if" combat triggers are left unmodeled.
- **Damage and drains.** "Deals N damage to each opponent / target opponent / any target / the player it's attacking", "each opponent loses N life", "damage equal to its power / that creature's power / the number of X", and X drains (Exsanguinate) hit opponents. A single target means the defending player in a combat trigger, otherwise the opponent closest to dying. "Each player" also costs you. Burn that can hit creatures ("any target" on an instant or sorcery) stays held interaction; triggers aimed at "any target" go face. Proliferate adds poison to poisoned opponents.
- **Disruption: two modes.** `--disruption auto` (default) runs the **ladder** when the bracket is 2, 3 or 4 (`# bracket:` header or `--bracket`), otherwise the **sampled** read. Never guess a bracket to get the ladder; ask.
- **Ladder** (`data/goldfish_gradients.json`). Per shuffle (100 by default, `--shuffles`): 5 clean baselines, then 15 rungs of the bracket's interaction ladder, on the same library order. That's 2,000 games; about 4 seconds on a normal list.
  - **Baselines** keep the library order but reseed the in-game randomness: tutor reshuffles, `~opp` coin flips, random effects. Their spread is the **noise band**. Every rung is compared against the shuffle's baseline **mean**, and a Δ smaller than the band is noise.
  - **Rungs** go from one late wipe (rung 1) to playing through the bracket's best control deck (rung 15). Each rung is the previous one plus one change: an added event, one moved earlier, or one upgraded. Events are `SLOT=code@T`, and a slot keeps its ID when it moves or upgrades.
  - **Firing.** Events don't all fire. Each slot rolls one number per shuffle, **once**, and fires at a rung if the roll is under `weight(code) × intensity(rung)`. That probability never decreases for a slot, so a slot that fired at rung 10 fires at every higher rung on that shuffle. The ladder stays nested and each shuffle's curve can be compared. A `!` (backed up: your first answer gets countered) is its own roll. Expected events at rung 15 are about 5 (B2), 5.8 (B3) and 7.5 (B4). `--ladder-max` fires everything; use it as a stress test or to audit the pilot, not as the read.
  - **Horizon** is where the scripted interaction ends: B2 T10, B3 T9, B4 T8 (`--horizon`). Games stop there, or earlier when the table dies (the win condition exists; there is no loss condition yet, since opponents never attack). A struggling deck is never cut short: the struggle is data.
  - **Codes:** `rem` (removal to graveyard; biggest permanent half the time), `rem+` (exile; key/tracked cards first, then highest MV), `cmd`, `wipe`, `nuke` (nonland wipe, Treasures too), `nuke+gy` (Farewell: exile nonland permanents and your graveyard), `rift` (your nonland permanents to hand, commander to the command zone; cleanup discard applies), `ctr4/ctr3/ctr2` (counter your first spell of that MV or more that turn, or your commander), `ctrK` (held from turn T until you cast your commander or a key/tracked card), `gy` (exile your graveyard), `ld` (destroy a land, nonbasic with the most colors first), `tax` (noncreature spells cost {1} more, Thalia), `taxall` (all spells, Sphere), `lock` (no casting from the command zone or graveyard; unearth still works; Drannith).
  - **Persistent effects.** `tax`/`taxall`/`lock` last 3 of your turns, or until the pilot kills the piece with held removal. Thalia and Drannith count as creatures and Sphere as an artifact. `--explain` shows "can kill tax/lock pieces" on held removal; that parse is approximate. Taxes also raise the cost of your held answers.
  - **Surviving hits.** Hexproof/shroud creatures can't be chosen by `cmd`/`rem`/`rem+` (answered; Alpha Authority). Destroy effects (`rem`, `wipe`, `nuke`, and `cmd` half the time, since commander removal is often exile) miss indestructible creatures, and umbra/totem armor destroys the Aura instead (answered for spot removal). Exile (`rem+`, `nuke+gy`) and `rift` ignore both.
  - **Answers.** Counters answer every spell-based event, including counters, tax and lock pieces, but not `gy` (Bojuka Bog) or `ld` (Strip Mine). Protection answers removal, wipes and rift; against `nuke+gy` it saves the board, but the graveyard is still exiled. Redirects answer targeted removal.
  - **Fold.** A game folds when a hit event pushed it below the deck's clean floor (P10 of resolved spells across all baselines) from at or above it, or cost 2+ commander turns against the shuffle's baseline mean. Both thresholds are set in the gradients file. Shuffles already below the floor when clean are reported separately and fold only on commander turns. A game that killed the table never folds (a fast kill resolves fewer spells; that isn't a collapse).
  - **Reading it:**
    - The rung table shows fired/hit events, answered %, Δ resolved spells/mana/extra cards/board/recursion/commander turns/dead turns (turns with nothing cast), rebuilt (share of games whose first board hit got back to the pre-hit board size by the horizon, with median turns), and fold %.
    - Below it, the **breakpoint**: per shuffle, the first rung that folds (P25/median/P75, plus never-folded %).
    - **"What changed at the breakpoint"** counts the events that were new or moved at each shuffle's breakpoint. That is the diagnosis: which interaction this deck folds to.
    - Δspells counts resolved spells. Positive Δspells or Δmana after hits means rebuilding with spare mana (recasts, commander tax); read that as resilience, not gain.
    - Rebuild reads are cut off by the horizon, so a late hit can't show a recovery.
    - Δdmg is the change in damage dealt to opponents; Δkill t is the change in the turn the table died (a game that didn't kill counts as horizon + 1); won is the share of games that killed the table. For a combat deck, Δkill t is the clock read: how many turns the interaction bought the table.
  - `--variant` adds ladder rows (median breakpoint, never folded, average and top-rung fold, top-rung Δcommander turns, top-rung Δkill turn).
  - `--disruption-trace S:R` prints shuffle S at rung R.
- **Sampled** (`--disruption sample`; the fallback when there's no ladder bracket). Every game is replayed on the same shuffle with 0-2 events: 25% none, 50% one, 25% two, on turns 3+. Events are commander removal, spot removal, creature wipe, nonland wipe, and a counterspell on your first MV 3+ spell (or commander). The table reports the paired average change per scenario against the same clean game.
- **Fixed** (`--disruption "wipe@5;cmd@4;ctrK!@3"`, any ladder code): every game faces the same events. Pair it with `--variant` to compare builds against the same hit. `--disruption-trace N` shows game N; `--disruption off` skips disruption.
- **All modes:**
  - Removed cards go to the graveyard (exile for `rem+`/`nuke+gy`), so recursion can rebuild.
  - Held counters, protection and redirects answer an event if you left the mana open, or for free with a commander out (Fierce Guardianship).
  - The pilot never taps out at end of turn to cycle below its cheapest held answer, but it doesn't hold mana in the main phase.
  - "Answered" is how often your interaction saved you; "no target" is how often there was nothing to hit (a slow start dodges wipes).
  - Commander turns lost is usually the headline.
- **Spells with a sacrifice cost and Skullclamp** (2026-09-30). "As an additional cost to cast this spell, sacrifice ..." (Deadly Dispute, Village Rites, Diabolic Intent, Bone Splinters) is paid with the same fodder order as activated abilities: Treasure, Food, Clues and tokens, never a real card, so with no fodder the spell can't be cast. A spell that puts a creature onto the battlefield may trade a small plain creature. Fling-style spells ("the sacrificed creature's power") throw the biggest creature, and only when it's lethal. "Whenever equipped/enchanted creature dies" triggers fire (Skullclamp, Bequeathal); before combat, clamp-style Equipment goes on creatures it kills that the pilot doesn't mind losing (tokens, fodder), again while mana lasts; equipping any creature to toughness 0 kills it. "Doesn't untap during your untap step" sources (Mana Vault, Grim Monolith) stay tapped and are spent last; paying at least what they make to untap them is never done.
- **Triggers also read** (2026-09-30): "whenever you draw a card" (every card drawn, including the draw step: Psychosis Crawler, Niv-Mizzet), "whenever a player casts" (yours and the ~opp casts; lines rewarding "that player" stay unread), "whenever an opponent draws, ~ deals damage to them". Intervening "if" on these stays unmodeled like the other frames. Filtered digs ("look at the top N, you may reveal a creature card from among them") take the best match, the rest go to the bottom; Ponder/Top-style "put them back in any order" arranges the best card on top.
- **Correctness pass** (2026-09-30, second session). "[You may] COST. If you do, EFFECT" pays the cost first: discard (worst card; none in hand, no effect), sacrifice ~, sacrifice fodder (the usual fodder order), pay N life (above the floor); "Otherwise" is the unpaid branch; an unreadable cost drops the effect instead of making it free (Formidable Speaker, Aegis Sculptor, Bitter Reunion). Pod-style searches ("mana value equal to 1 plus the sacrificed creature's": Birthing Pod, Neoform, Eldritch Evolution, Prime Speaker Vannifar) are capped by the sacrificed permanent, which must have a target one step up and no ongoing ability; a search's destination in the next sentence is read (Eldritch Evolution enters the battlefield). Converge and sunburst count the colors of the units that paid (~approx: the payment isn't chosen to maximize colors). Cascade casts the first nonland card with lesser MV free; the rest go to the bottom. Your creatures with toughness 0 or less die (CR 704.5f; not X/X tokens or unread */* bodies). "Target player mills" mills you only with a graveyard payoff. "Where X is ..." is read anywhere in the ability (Tezzeret's "You gain X life"); devotion is a count (Gray Merchant, with "gain the life lost"). "Activate only if you control N ..." mana (Mox Opal's metalcraft, Fanatic of Rhonas) works only while it holds. Self-fetching lands (Riveteers Overlook) fetch on entering. -1/-1 counters on "target creature you control" go where they hurt least (Soulstinger).
- **Mana, counts and doublers** (2026-09-30, second session). Sacrifice outlets (Ashnod's Altar, Phyrexian Altar/Tower, Krark-Clan Ironworks, Gilded Goose, Skirk Prospector) turn cheap fodder (tokens up to power 3, Food, Treasure, Clues; never a real card or the commander) into mana the solver spends last and never pours into X; dies and sacrifice triggers fire, a tapping outlet gives one creature per turn, and fodder mana isn't counted as development. "X, where X is ..." is read wherever it appears in the ability (Krenko makes one Goblin per Goblin; the pilot taps him when the tokens beat his attack). Rituals that count ("Add {R} for each Goblin"), "N mana in any combination of {U}, {B}, and/or {R}", conditional mana ("Activate only if you control three or more artifacts": Mox Opal, Fanatic of Rhonas), Chrome Mox (imprints the least useful card of a color the lands lack), Mox Diamond (needs and discards a spare land). Token doublers (Doubling Season, Anointed Procession, Parallel Lives; Ojer Taq triples creature tokens) and trigger doublers, by cause (Panharmonicon, Yarok, Teysa, Isshin, Ancient Greenwarden) or by source (Roaming Throne, Annie Joins Up); self-ETBs double too.
- **Tribes, sagas, copies, kicker, conditions** (2026-09-30, second session). "The chosen type" is the deck's tribe: its most common creature type (commanders count twice, three creatures needed), else it matches nothing (Herald's Horn, Urza's Incubator, Vanquisher's Banner, Metallic Mimic, Roaming Throne). Sagas follow CR 714: a lore counter as one enters and as each precombat main phase begins (and from proliferate; Doubling Season skips a chapter), each chapter triggers once, and the Saga is sacrificed after its last (Urza's Saga: its granted abilities are read as its own, ~approx; chapter III finds an artifact of MV 1 or less, then the land is gone). Token copies of itself, "that creature", the equipped creature (Helm of the Host), your best target (Kiki-Jiki, Rite of Replication: a creature with an ETB first) or each match (Second Harvest), with haste and "sacrifice/exile it at the beginning of the next end step". Kicker and multikicker are paid when the pool covers them (before X); "if ~ was kicked" holds only for the cast card, kicked "... instead" sentences replace the base effect. Intervening "if" conditions are read: "if you control N or more / no / another [filter]", life, hand size, raid ("if you attacked this turn": only after combat), graveyard counts, "if you cast it", your turn / main phase. Any "If ...," sentence whose condition isn't read has its effect dropped and the line marked partial: it is never applied unconditionally. Trigger filters keep their qualifiers (power N or less, a keyword, non-X, a counter, modified, equipped, colors, legendary); a filter with words it can't read leaves the trigger unread instead of firing for any creature. Black Market Connections' modes are each skipped when their life cost would cross the floor. Harrow / Crop Rotation sacrifice a tapped land; Natural Order a green creature; Victimize a small body for two.
- **Audit fixes** (2026-09-30, third session). Found by sampling 40 modeled cards and reading every changed line of the diff against Oracle text.
  - **Counts.** "Loses/gains N life for each X" scales with X (Last Stand's 2 per Swamp, Filigree Angel's 3 per artifact). An unread "for each" or "where X is" leaves the line partial instead of dropping the amount silently (Search for Glory, Dwynen). A count reads only what it names: "colorless creature you control", "nonland permanents", "basic Islands", "artifact and/or enchantment" (either), "artifact creature" (both). Anything else it can't read stays unread. It never falls back to "creatures you control".
  - **Symmetric loot.** "Each player / target player draws N, then discards N" (Geier Reach Sanitarium, Cephalid Coliseum, Dack Fayden) discards too.
  - **"Loses that much life"** is the life just gained only inside a "whenever you gain life" trigger (Sanguine Bond, Vito). Vizkopa Guildmage's delayed trigger and Caustic Bronco's revealed card stay unread.
  - **Copies.** An "except" clause that changes what the token is (Astral Dragon's 3/3 Dragons, Mishra's 4/4 Warform, Jace's loyalty 1) leaves the copy unread. "Isn't legendary", "has haste", an extra keyword or type, and a quoted "at the beginning of the end step, sacrifice/exile this token" are read (Electroduplicate, Heat Shimmer, Minion Reflector now go away at the end step).
  - **Conditions.** "Attacks alone" and "~ and at least N other creatures attack" (battalion) are conditions, including granted ones (Idolized, Voltaic Whip). "No other X" doesn't count ~ itself (Dust Stalker). "An artifact and an enchantment" needs both (Naomi). Fateful hour ("5 or less life") and addendum ("during your main phase") are read. An ability word in front of an "If" (adamant, spell mastery, raid, threshold, ferocious) no longer hides the condition. Adamant, delirium and revolt stay unread, so their effects drop instead of always applying.
  - **Opponent sentences.** A sentence whose subject is an opponent or the owner of target permanent (Chaos Warp's reveal, Dusk Mangler's discard, "target player discards") isn't counted against the line.
  - **Tribes.** "The chosen type" is the deck's tribe only when the card says "choose a creature type". Cloud Key's card type, Dawn-Blessed Pennant's four types and Creeping Renaissance's permanent type stay unread.
- **Sacrifice costs and the untap engine** (the Ragost, Deft Gastronaut work of 2026-09-30).
  - **Activated abilities** used in main phase 2, after everything castable is cast: card flow (draw, look, tutor, land search, recursion, proliferate), Treasure and token makers (Nuka-Cola's Food, Clue makers), damage or drain to opponents, and life gain when it untaps an engine (below). They run in passes, fodder makers first, so a Food made in one pass feeds a sacrifice in the next. Non-tap abilities repeat at most 3 times a turn. "Activate only as a sorcery" is read.
  - **"Sacrifice a Food / another creature / an artifact or creature / two artifacts"** costs are read (a qualified one like "another colorless creature", or a land, leaves the ability unmodeled). The pilot pays with the cheapest fodder: tokens whose leaving pays off (Munitions), then tapped Food/Treasure (they can't use their own {T} ability this turn), untapped Food, designed fodder (Prized Statue, Ichor Wellspring: they pay off when they hit the graveyard), untapped Treasure, Clue, then creature tokens by power. Real cards (engine pieces, mana rocks, Equipment in use) and the commander are never fodder, unless the ability kills an opponent. A creature is fodder only when the ability is worth at least three of its attacks (Ragost's 9 damage for a 1/1 Servo) or kills: Goblin Bombardment keeps a 1/1 attacker. A "sacrifice ~" ability granted to a real card (Ragost's Food grant on Nuka-Cola) follows the same rule. The fodder's own mana isn't spent on the cost. An ability dealing "the sacrificed creature's power" sees the fodder.
  - **Type grants.** "Artifacts / creatures / permanents you control are Foods (or artifacts...) in addition to their other types and have \"...\"" adds those types for fodder costs, sacrifice triggers and filters, and every permanent it covers can use the quoted ability, read like card text (Ragost: each artifact gets "{2}, {T}, Sacrifice this artifact: You gain 3 life"). Treasures are artifacts, so under Ragost a Treasure cashed in for mana is a Food and Nuka-Cola replaces it with a tapped Treasure.
  - **The untap engine.** "At the beginning of each end step, if you gained life this turn, untap ~" is read (as are upkeep/end triggers conditioned on "if you have N or more life", e.g. Test of Endurance's win). A card with it and a {T} damage ability is an engine. On each opponent's turn, before their end step, it activates at instant speed from the mana you left open (activations with instant=True: only damage abilities and the life gain that untaps them; no sorcery-speed or loyalty abilities). When the engine is tapped, no life has been gained this turn and an opponent turn is still to come, the pilot uses a life-gain ability (a Food's, or the one Ragost grants to an untapped Treasure) if mana is left for the next activation. Lifelink or Spirit Loop makes every activation untap it. On your turn the engine's mana is held for the opponents' turns (its cost times the opponents left): other activations, paid triggers ("you may pay {2}. If you do", Well of Lost Dreams' X) and end-of-turn cycling leave it open; the engine, the life gain and fodder makers don't. Spells in main phases 1 and 2 still come first. Ragost + Nuka-Cola + Basilisk Collar with 5 mana deals 3 to each opponent on all 4 turns of a round (unit check).
  - **Staying home.** A creature with a {T} damage ability worth at least its power stays out of combat when the mana and fodder are there (fodder counts if an untapped permanent can make it, like Nuka-Cola's Food).
  - **Combat rows cover the round.** The combat and damage rows for turn T include the opponents' turns after your turn T (engine activations, ~opp drains), and a table killed on an opponent's turn ends the game in that round.
- **Not modeled:** opponents' attacks, opposing creatures beyond `--blockers` (none by default; the blocker gradient comes next), Rogue's Passage-style activated evasion, damage to each opposing creature (Hazardous Blast's ping), your own symmetrical wipes (held, never cast), -X/-0 power debuffs, exert (Combat Celebrant), "untap target creature" (Seize the Day's untap), opponents' graveyards, opponents' sacrifices, land fodder for mana (Orcish Lumberjack), non-mana kicker costs ("Kicker—Sacrifice ..."), storm, morph and the other keywords `--explain` lists as not modeled, activated abilities in the graveyard (Cauldron Familiar), "exile a creature" costs (Food Chain), damage doublers on damage to your own creatures, copies that aren't tokens (Clone effects), noncreature tokens other than Treasure/Clue/Food/Gold and named artifact tokens, copies, dredge, delve, disturb/embalm/eternalize/encore/aftermath, graveyard costs that aren't mana/discard/exile-from-graveyard (Dread Return's sacrifice), "for each card in your graveyard" counts, and anything the explain list marks partial or blank. Say so when these matter to the question.
- **Tokens.** Creature tokens enter as permanents with their power, toughness, keywords ("with flying") and subtypes (they count for board size, "greatest power", creature counts and enter triggers, attack the turn after they enter unless they have haste, and vanish when they leave). Tokens created "tapped and attacking" join the current attack at the same defending player without triggering attack triggers. A quoted ability on the token is read like card text (Eldrazi Scion mana). Treasures, Clues ({2}, sacrifice: draw), Food ({2}, {T}, sacrifice: gain 3) and Gold are artifact tokens on the battlefield; investigate makes a Clue. Treasures are spent for mana after single-color sources, can enter tapped ("create a tapped Treasure"), count as artifacts (affinity, "for each artifact"), and don't count toward the board size in the report or the rebuild read. Named artifact tokens with a quoted ability (Weapons Manufacturing's Munitions) and artifact creature tokens (Servo, Thopter) are artifacts too. Token replacements: Academy Manufactor turns a Clue, Food or Treasure into one of each (n Manufactors: 3^(n-1) of each); Stridehangar Automaton adds a 1/1 flying Thopter per artifact-token event. Spot removal (disruption) never picks a noncreature token. "For each X" counts use the same keys as draw ("for each creature you control"); a count the sim can't see is skipped. Tokens made off an opponent's creature (Kalitas, Beast Within) are skipped. A cap of 150 permanents stops runaway loops.
- **Mana scaling.** Multipliers ("three times as much": Nyxbloom, Mana Reflection) and per-tap extras (Vorinclex, Zendikar Resurgent, Crypt Ghast) apply to every source they name; one entering mid-turn rescales untapped sources. Variable abilities read their count when tapped: greatest power (Selvala), "for each Elf" (Priest of Titania), creatures (Cradle), devotion.
- **Reducers and free casting.** Filters read card types, colors, real subtypes (from the CR 205.3 list: "Dragon spells"), power floors (Goreclaw), and "the first creature spell each turn" (Shadow in the Warp). "~ costs {1} less for each X" reads the same count keys. "Cast X spells without paying" takes a filter; without "from your hand" it covers the command zone too (Dracogenesis on a Dragon commander; tax still paid). Affinity ("affinity for artifacts"/"for Xs") and self-reducers counting your graveyard (all cards, creature cards, land cards, instants and sorceries) are read; qualified counts ("attacking creature", "creature with a counter", "creature type among") are left unread. Convoke and improvise are flagged in `--explain`, not modeled.
- **X-tutors** (Nature's Rhythm, Chord, GSZ, Finale) only find cards with mana value at most the X paid. The pilot pays all spare mana into X.
- **Modal spells** ("Choose one —") use the best goldfish mode: draw, then look/tutor/recur, land search, Treasure, tokens, mana. "One or both"/"two" take the top two.
- **Opponent landfall** ("whenever a land an opponent controls enters") is a fixed ~opp approximation: it fires on each opponent turn while their land count is still growing.
- Upkeep/end-step triggers with an intervening "if" are left unmodeled and shown in `--explain`, not fired every turn, except "if an opponent controls more lands" (~opp), "if you gained life this turn" and "if you have N or more life". A read one that fails its check doesn't count as a trigger fired.
- Conditional upgrades written as "... instead if" (threshold on Cabal Ritual) are modeled at their **base** effect only, and the upgrade line shows as unmodeled: Cabal Ritual makes BBB, not BBBBB. Kicker, raid, threshold, ferocious, spell mastery, fateful hour and addendum sentences are read as conditions. Adamant, delirium and revolt are not, so their effect is dropped.
- Permanents that grant extra land drops (Exploration, Azusa) give them the turn they enter.

## Fixing a card

**Parser changes** follow `docs/GOLDFISH_ROADMAP.md`: `scripts/goldfish_coverage.py diff HEAD` shows every card in the Commander pool whose reading changed; check each against Oracle text before pushing. `goldfish_coverage.py card NAME` explains any card without a deck file.

**Fixing a card: `data/goldfish_overrides.json`.** Key = Oracle name. An entry replaces only the fields it names; always add a `note`. Fields: `mana` (list of `{"colors": "any" | "WU" | "C", "count": n, "restrict": "legendary", "colored_only": false, "sick": true}`), `etb`, `spell` (lists of effect strings), `triggers` (list of `{"on": "cast|etb|landfall|upkeep|end|drawstep|prolif|cycle|opp_cast|opp_draw|opp_second|combat_begin|attack|attack_self|attack_any|cdmg|cdmg_self|cdmg_any|attack_att|cdmg_att|dies|dies_self|gy_self|leave_self|sac|gain|dmg_att|dmg_self", "filter": "noncreature", "do": [...], "once": false, "tax": false, "each": false}`; the filter is a spell filter for cast events and a permanent filter such as "creature" or "Dragon" for etb/attack/cdmg/dies/sac), `activated` (list of `{"cost": "{2}", "tap": true, "sac": false, "fodder": "Food", "remove": "divinity 1", "do": [...]}`; `fodder` is a sacrifice-another-permanent cost in Oracle words, e.g. "artifact or creature"), `pt` (`[power, toughness]`), `keywords` (`["flying", "toxic 2"]`, which replaces the read keywords), `hold`, `requires`, `cat`, `skip`, `status`. Effect strings: `draw 2`, `draw permanents` (also lands, creatures, artifacts, power, colors, opp_hand), `scry 2`, `surveil 1`, `look 3 1`, `prolif 1`, `treasure 1`, `extra_land 1`, `land_from_hand 1`, `land basic bf_t 1`, `ctr divinity 1`, `mana WUBRG`, `tutor DEST TARGET` and `recur DEST TARGET` (DEST `hand`/`top`/`bf`/`graveyard`; TARGET in Oracle words, e.g. `recur hand a creature card`), `mill 3`, `face 2` (one opponent loses 2), `face_each 1`, `life 3` (you gain 3; negative loses), `pump 2 0 self trample` (until end of turn; `self`/`obj`/`target`; multi-word keywords with underscores: `pump 0 0 obj double_strike`), `pump_team 1 1 trample`. When a parse miss affects many cards, fix the parser in `goldfish.py` instead of piling up overrides.
