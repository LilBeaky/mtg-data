# GOLDFISH — goldfish.py

Companion to `USE_INSTRUCTIONS.md` §6. Read this only when running `goldfish.py`. This doc is expected to change as the simulator is tested and iterated on.


Plays the deck alone thousands of times with a greedy pilot and reports development and card flow. **Scope: the deck in a vacuum.** Opponents are never simulated as decision-makers; disruption to your own game is (see Disruption). Use it for timing questions ("when is the commander out?", "when does the payoff land?"), color reliability, card-advantage reads, and comparing swaps. Stats Math stays the tool for pure draw odds.

```
python3 scripts/goldfish.py DECK [--turns 8] [--trials 2000] [--draw] [--seed 1]
    [--track "Label=REGEX"] [--variant "Label|Out=>In;Out=>In"] [--kill-commander T]
    [--order commander,track,ramp,draw,other] [--disruption sample|off|"wipe@5;cmd@4"] [--disruption-trace N]
    [--cast-interaction] [--no-mulligan] [--explain] [--trace N] [--json]
```

- **Always run `--explain` first** on a new list. Each card is marked `modeled`, `partial` (some lines unread), `blank` (cast for its cost, does nothing), `held` (removal/counters/protection instants and sorceries, never cast proactively; counters and protection can answer disruption), `vacuum` (its text needs opponents, so it does nothing here: Tithe Taker, Archivist of Oghma), or `override`. `~opp` marks cards running on the fixed opponent approximations below. Tell the user which important cards are partial, blank, vacuum or ~opp before quoting numbers.
- `--track "Myojin=^Myojin of"` reports the first turn a card group is cast or put onto the battlefield (tutored or reanimated counts; a bare card name works too). Tracked cards get cast priority right after the commander, and tutors fetch them first.
- `--variant` runs a swapped build on the **same shuffles** and prints a side-by-side table. Use it for every cut-vs-add question. It's far less noisy than two separate runs. `--explain --variant ...` also lists the incoming cards.
- `--kill-commander T` removes the commander after turn T-1 in every clean game (recast with tax; a held answer can stop it). For a deliberate "what if" on the main report. The disruption section covers this sampled.
- `--trace N` prints a play-by-play of game N. Use it to audit the pilot whenever a number looks off.
- Speed: about 2,000 games per build in 6 to 8 seconds with the disruption replay (half that with `--disruption off`).

**What the report means.** A `tutor priorities` header line means the pilot read `# key:` / `# package:` lines (see Tutors below). Development rows give P10/median/P90. P10 is the floor, which the user cares about most. "Mana" counts sources available at the start of the main phase, and "all colors" counts restricted mana (e.g. Plaza of Heroes' legendary-only colors) as available. "Extra cards" are cards put into hand beyond draw steps, counted net: a wheel counts cards drawn minus the hand it threw away, and looting or Brainstorm-style put-backs subtract what leaves your hand, so they show as card filtering rather than card advantage. "Stranded" counts spells you had the mana *amount* for but not the colors. "Discarded" is cleanup discard, a flood signal. "Extra cards by source" names the card-advantage engines. "Graveyard" is its size at end of turn; "recursion" counts cards back from the graveyard (to hand, to the battlefield, or cast from it), with "recursion by source" naming them; "cycled" counts cycling and typecycling. "Tutor targets" lists what tutors actually fetched: read it to check the pilot is tutoring the way the user would.

**Model and fixed conventions.**
- London mulligan with the free first mulligan (rule 103.5c). Keep 3–5 lands, or 2 lands plus a cheap ramp piece.
- **Opponent approximations (fixed, no flags).** Cards that only work off other players get a best approximation, tagged `~opp`: 3 other turns a round (a 4-player pod: "each upkeep/end step" triggers fire there); each opponent draws 1 and casts 1 spell a turn (40% creatures), a second 30% of the time (Kraum, Monologue Tax); taxes (Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, Esper Sentinel) are paid 75% of the time, so each lands about once a round; an opponent holds 4 cards (Windfall); an opponent's land count is their turn count ("if an opponent controls more lands than you": Land Tax, Knight of the White Orchid, Gift of Estates). Other "if an opponent has more X" conditions do nothing. Cumulative upkeep permanents are let go after 3 upkeeps. Never model what opponents choose: what they cast for synergy, their extra draws, reanimating from their graveyards.
- Summoning sickness, enters-tapped rules (fast/slow/check/reveal/shock/battlebond), fetches, bounce lands, filter lands and converters, restricted mana, colored-only mana, alt costs (Jodah, Fist of Suns), Omniscience-style free casting, cost reducers, extra land drops, rituals (cast only when they enable a spell), X-draw spells (X ≥ 2), counters with Hardened Scales/Doubling Season-style modifiers, proliferate, remove-a-counter draw abilities (keeps one divinity/indestructible counter), planeswalker loyalty, activated draw/proliferate/tutor abilities, triggers on cast/ETB/landfall/upkeep/draw step/end step/proliferate, rebound, and leylines are all modeled.
- **Tutors** read their target and destination with `tutors.py`'s parser, so the two tools agree (MV limits, power, subtypes, "or" alternatives, transmute's same-MV). Destinations: hand, top, battlefield (tapped or not), graveyard, castable exile; exile with no way to cast does nothing. A target with words the parser can't read leaves the ability unused; other approximations (⚠ in tutors.py) are used and shown with `~` in `--explain`. The pilot fetches, in order: a `# key:` card not yet in hand or play; the missing piece of a `# package:` (more when the rest is assembled); a tutor that can reach one of those; then its general sense of value. It fetches a land only when short, avoids a second copy of something it has, and marks down cards it can't cast soon. Graveyard tutors (Entomb, Buried Alive) are cast only with a payoff: recursion in hand, play or library, or a flashback-style card castable from there. They pick a reanimation target first. They are never counted as card advantage.
- **Graveyard.** Discards, resolved spells, sacrifices, surveil, mill, cycling and "reveal N, take one, rest into your graveyard" fill it. Recursion to hand or battlefield (Regrowth, Reanimate, Exhume, Sheoldred's upkeep, ETB returners, Animate Dead-style Auras) and casting from it (flashback, jump-start, retrace, escape, unearth, harmonize's base cost) are modeled. Flashback/jump-start/escape spells are exiled after; retrace goes back; unearth is exiled at end step. Reanimation onto the battlefield waits for a target of MV 4+ (anything from turn 6). Only your own graveyard exists.
- **Cycling family.** Cycling (mana or life), typecycling/landcycling and transmute are hand abilities, with "when you cycle ~" riders. The pilot landcycles for a missed land drop, transmutes toward a key/package card, and spends leftover mana at end of turn cycling dead cards (more freely with a cycle payoff on the battlefield). Cycling is filtering: net 0 extra cards.
- **Life** starts at 40. The pilot won't pay life below 20 (Griselbrand, life-cost cycling) and Reanimate-style life loss counts. Other life gain and loss isn't tracked.
- **Disruption.** After the clean run, every game is replayed on the same shuffle with a sampled scenario (`--disruption sample`, the default): 25% none, 50% one event, 25% two, on turns 3+. Events: commander removal (to the command zone, recast with tax), spot removal (your biggest nonland permanent half the time, a random one otherwise, to the graveyard), creature wipe, nonland wipe (Treasures too), and a counterspell on your first MV 3+ spell (or commander) that turn. Removed cards go to the graveyard, so recursion can rebuild. Your held counters (any event), protection (not counters) and redirects (targeted removal only) answer an event if you left the mana open, or for free with a commander out (Fierce Guardianship). The pilot never taps out at end of turn to cycle below its cheapest held answer, but it doesn't hold mana in the main phase. The table reports, per scenario, the paired average change against the same clean game: spells, mana spent, extra cards, board size, recursion, and **commander turns lost** (usually the headline). "Answered" is how often your interaction saved you; "no target" is how often there was nothing to hit (a slow start dodges wipes). Positive Δspells/Δmana after a hit means rebuilding with spare mana: resilience, not a gain. Use a fixed scenario (`"wipe@5"`) to compare builds against the same hit with `--variant`; `--disruption-trace N` shows one replay; `--disruption off` skips it.
- **Not modeled:** combat, opponents' graveyards, tokens other than Treasures, copies, dredge, delve, disturb/embalm/eternalize/encore/aftermath, graveyard costs that aren't mana/discard/exile-from-graveyard (Dread Return's sacrifice), "for each card in your graveyard" counts, and anything the explain list marks partial or blank. Say so when these matter to the question.
- Conditional upgrades ("...instead if" threshold, kicker, delirium, addendum lines) are modeled at their **base** effect only and the upgrade line shows as unmodeled. Cabal Ritual makes BBB, not BBBBB.
- Permanents that grant extra land drops (Exploration, Azusa) give them the turn they enter.

## Fixing a card

**Fixing a card: `data/goldfish_overrides.json`.** Key = Oracle name. An entry replaces only the fields it names; always add a `note`. Fields: `mana` (list of `{"colors": "any" | "WU" | "C", "count": n, "restrict": "legendary", "colored_only": false, "sick": true}`), `etb`, `spell` (lists of effect strings), `triggers` (list of `{"on": "cast|etb|landfall|upkeep|end|drawstep|prolif|cycle|opp_cast|opp_draw|opp_second", "filter": "noncreature", "do": [...], "once": false, "tax": false, "each": false}`), `activated` (list of `{"cost": "{2}", "tap": true, "sac": false, "remove": "divinity 1", "do": [...]}`), `hold`, `requires`, `cat`, `skip`, `status`. Effect strings: `draw 2`, `draw permanents` (also lands, creatures, artifacts, power, colors, opp_hand), `scry 2`, `surveil 1`, `look 3 1`, `prolif 1`, `treasure 1`, `extra_land 1`, `land_from_hand 1`, `land basic bf_t 1`, `ctr divinity 1`, `mana WUBRG`, `tutor DEST TARGET` and `recur DEST TARGET` (DEST `hand`/`top`/`bf`/`graveyard`; TARGET in Oracle words, e.g. `recur hand a creature card`), `mill 3`. When a parse miss affects many cards, fix the parser in `goldfish.py` instead of piling up overrides.
